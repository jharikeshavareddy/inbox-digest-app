Inbox Digest

Inbox Digest is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jakka Harikeshava Reddy. It runs on the owner's own computer (self-hosted n8n) and builds a private daily summary of the owner's own email inboxes and calendars.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

It requests read-only access (Gmail read-only, Calendar read-only). It never sends, deletes or changes email or calendar events.
